Introducing The American Surge
 

We are thrilled to officially introduce The American Surge, a newly launched auxiliary ministry of Firebrand Revivalists. We chose the name because of the significance of the meaning of Surge being a strong forward movement which is needed in our nation. The ideas from the Lord that we have for this movement are deeply rooted in my calling to America. But as everyone knows, everything in the Kingdom begins with prayer. And this is why we are building The American Surge this way — beginning with prayer.

While The American Surge encompasses a much broader vision, we are beginning with America’s Watchmen, a ministry dedicated to mobilizing, equipping, and uniting believers in strategic, biblically grounded prayer for our nation.

Our heart is to cultivate a community of spiritually mature intercessors who understand the authority of God’s Word, the power of agreement, and the responsibility we carry as believers to stand in the gap for our families, communities, and nation.

There are several additional exciting endeavors we are pursuing under The American Surge. We look forward to sharing more about those initiatives with you when the time is right.

Continuing Our National Leadership with the Nehemiah Alliance in 2027
 

We are also excited to announce that we have been invited back to serve as National Leaders with the Nehemiah Alliance for 2027.

It was a tremendous privilege to serve alongside national ministry leaders throughout the 52-Day Rebuild in 2026, culminating in the Sacred Assembly on the National Mall in Washington, D.C.

We are grateful for the opportunity to continue serving alongside leaders who share a passion for seeing America return to God, strengthening the Church, restoring families, and mobilizing believers across our nation in unified prayer.

We believe there is great significance in the relationships God is establishing among His people in this hour. As we look toward 2027, we are filled with expectation for what the Lord will accomplish through the power of unity, obedience, and agreement.

We look forward to sharing more details with you as plans for the coming year unfold.

A Power-Packed Prayer Push
 

On November 23, we launched a concentrated prayer initiative through The American Surge | America’s Watchmen, committing two to three hours each day to praying in the Holy Spirit. We will continue this prayer push through December 1, and it is not too late to join us.

Participants are praying in the power of the Holy Spirit from the comfort of their homes, on their own schedules, while standing together in the power of agreement. We are pressing in for our families, our nation, the Church, and the advancement of the Kingdom of God.

There is tremendous power in the body of Christ coming together in unity, faith, and fervent prayer.

Building a Digital Home for America’s Watchmen
 

Many years ago, a prophetic word was spoken over us concerning something we would build. Today, we believe we are beginning to see the unfolding of that promise.

We are now in the early stages of developing our own independent social platform specifically designed for those called to stand as watchmen over America.

Our vision is to create an innovative, intuitive, and user-friendly platform that will provide:

•The ability to track prayer taking place around the world for America.
•A dedicated community where intercessors can connect, communicate, and encourage one another.
•Biblically grounded teachings, training, and spiritual equipping.
•Strategic prayer assignments, prophetic declarations, and national prayer initiatives.
•A centralized place for believers to unite in prayer across geographical boundaries.

Imagine being able to see prayer taking place around the world for our nation, knowing that regardless of where you are, you are standing in agreement with others before the throne of God.

We believe this platform has the potential to strengthen, equip, and mobilize a global community of intercessors while providing the infrastructure necessary to support a growing prayer movement. There are many other initiatives this platform will serve in relation to America, and we will share them when the time is right.

Harnessing Technology for the Kingdom of God
 

Another exciting area in which we have been growing by leaps and bounds is artificial intelligence, and learning how to strategically utilize this technology for the advancement of the Kingdom of God.

Many years ago, a prophet spoke over us that God would give us new technology. At the time, we could not fully comprehend what that would look like. Today, as we continue to develop our understanding of AI and its extraordinary capabilities, we believe this may be part of the fulfillment of that prophetic word.

We are learning how to utilize these emerging technologies to expand our reach, develop resources, build infrastructure, strengthen ministry operations, and equip the body of Christ more effectively. We believe that God wants to use AI to protect the vulnerable from it being in the wrong hands. We are convinced the right people need to be learning this technology now to thwart what the wrong people would desire to do with it.

We believe there is tremendous opportunity for the body of Christ to steward technological advancements with wisdom, discernment, creativity, integrity, and excellence.

Technology is a tool, not a substitute for the Holy Spirit. Our desire is to use these resources under His leadership and for the glory of God.

“And I have filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship.”
Exodus 31:3

We are excited to see what the Lord will continue to reveal as we move forward.

Remember the Power of Communion
 

We also want to encourage you to continue taking communion, both individually and with your families.

Communion is a sacred remembrance of the finished work of Jesus Christ. It reminds us of His sacrifice, the covenant established through His blood, and the redemption we have received through Him.

Make communion a regular part of your household. Gather with your family, examine your hearts before the Lord, forgive one another, give thanks, pray together, and remember what Jesus accomplished on the cross.

“This do in remembrance of me.”
Luke 22:19
“For as often as ye eat this bread, and drink this cup, ye do shew the Lord’s death till he come.”
1 Corinthians 11:26
“The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not the communion of the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is it not the communion of the body of Christ?”
1 Corinthians 10:16

Additional Scriptures for reflection: Matthew 26:26–28, 1 Corinthians 11:23–29, and Isaiah 53:4–5.
