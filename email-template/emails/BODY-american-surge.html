Introducing The American Surge
 

We are thrilled to officially introduce The American Surge, a newly launched auxiliary ministry of Firebrand Revivalists. We chose the name because of the significance of the meaning of Surge being a strong forward movement which is needed in our nation. The ideas from the Lord that we have for this movement are deeply rooted in my calling to America. But as everyone knows, everything in the Kingdom begins with prayer. And this is why we are building The American Surge this way — beginning with prayer.

Coming Full Circle: A Return to Our Roots

Many years ago, our ministry began on social media with a passion to raise up and equip people in the power of prayer. Our heart was to train believers to hear the voice of God, become effective intercessors, and stand in the gap for their families and communities. Over the years, the Lord expanded our reach, leading us into national prayer initiatives and mobilizing believers to intercede for America.

Now, through The American Surge, God has brought us full circle — back to our roots, but with the wisdom, experience, and expanded vision cultivated over many years of ministry.

We believe there is a tremendous need in this hour to raise up spiritually discerning, equipped, and effective watchmen who understand that revival begins in our hearts, restoration begins in our homes, and the transformation of a nation begins with a praying people.

While The American Surge encompasses a much broader vision, we are beginning with America’s Watchmen, a ministry dedicated to mobilizing, equipping, and uniting believers in strategic, biblically grounded prayer for our nation.

Our heart is to cultivate a community of spiritually mature intercessors who understand the authority of God’s Word, the power of agreement, and the responsibility we carry as believers to stand in the gap for our families, communities, and nation.

There are several additional exciting endeavors we are pursuing under The American Surge. We look forward to sharing more about those initiatives with you when the time is right.

Building a Digital Home for America’s Watchmen
 

Many years ago, a prophetic word was spoken over us concerning something we would build. Today, we believe we are beginning to see the unfolding of that promise.

We are now in the early stages of developing our own independent social platform specifically designed for those called to stand as watchmen over America.

Our vision is to create an innovative, intuitive, and user-friendly platform that will provide:

•The ability to track prayer taking place around the world for America.
•A dedicated community where intercessors can connect, communicate, and encourage one another.
•Biblically grounded teachings, training, and spiritual equipping.
•Strategic prayer assignments, prophetic declarations, and national prayer initiatives.
•A centralized place for believers to unite in prayer across geographical boundaries.

Imagine being able to see prayer taking place around the world for our nation, knowing that regardless of where you are, you are standing in agreement with others before the throne of God.

We believe this platform has the potential to strengthen, equip, and mobilize a global community of intercessors while providing the infrastructure necessary to support a growing prayer movement. There are many other initiatives this platform will serve in relation to America, and we will share them when the time is right.

Revival Begins in the Home
 

One of the mantles upon Firebrand Revivalists is seeing children and families encounter the presence of God, to see the hearts of the fathers turned back to their children and the hearts of the children back to their fathers, and to see households become dwelling places of His presence.

This morning, we received a beautiful testimony that exemplifies the very heart of our ministry. One of our members who signed up for the prayer push, Lindsay Bartik, sent me this testimony about her daughter. Little Bea, rather than simply participating in worship with her mother, began leading their time in the presence of the Lord.

She instructed her mother to lie down and said:

“God is going to baptize you in the waters, let them flow over you, let it wash over you, He is baptizing you right now. Hallelujah!”

Her mother shared that, ordinarily, Bea would remain engaged for about five minutes before moving on to something else. But this morning was different. There was a reverence as they worshipped, prayed, and read Scripture together for approximately twenty minutes. Her mother recognized the beautiful work of the Holy Spirit taking place in her daughter since joining with the ministry.

Never underestimate what the Holy Spirit can do in and through your children.

Through our K–12 school chaplaincy ministry, Jesus Loves the Little Children, we carry this same passion into the lives of students, educators, and families. Our desire is not simply to teach children about God, but to cultivate opportunities for them to personally encounter Him, recognize His voice, develop a life of prayer, and grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ.

We are witnessing tremendous movement in some of the young men we are dealing with in the high school right now. The Lord is starting to open dreams to them that we are interpreting, and it is setting them free. We are helping many students who have been dealing with dark thoughts, and we are able to give them the tools to easily overcome. We believe this movement will be critical to what God desires to do in the schools across America in the future. Please keep this work in prayer as we continue to build the blueprints with the Holy Spirit.

We believe God is raising up a generation of children who will know His presence, walk in His Word, and demonstrate the power and love of the Holy Spirit.

“All your children shall be taught by the LORD, and great shall be the peace of your children.”
Isaiah 54:13

We want to encourage every person to intentionally gather with their families. Pray together. Worship together. Read the Word of God together. Take communion together. Make room for your children to participate and give them opportunities to pray and minister as the Holy Spirit leads them.

Be intentional about loving one another, restoring relationships, and cultivating an atmosphere in your home where the presence of God is welcomed and treasured.

We are not only contending for the restoration of our nation; we are contending for the restoration of the very foundation of our nation — the family.

May the altars of prayer be rebuilt in our homes. May the hearts of our children burn with love for Jesus. May marriages and families be strengthened, and may the presence of God become the centerpiece of our households once again.

Let the revival we are praying for across America begin within the walls of our own homes.

“And he will turn the hearts of the fathers to the children, and the hearts of the children to their fathers.”
Malachi 4:6
Remember the Power of Communion
 

We also want to encourage you to continue taking communion, both individually and with your families.

Communion is a sacred remembrance of the finished work of Jesus Christ. It reminds us of His sacrifice, the covenant established through His blood, and the redemption we have received through Him.

Make communion a regular part of your household. Gather with your family, examine your hearts before the Lord, forgive one another, give thanks, pray together, and remember what Jesus accomplished on the cross.

“This do in remembrance of me.”
Luke 22:19
“For as often as ye eat this bread, and drink this cup, ye do shew the Lord’s death till he come.”
1 Corinthians 11:26
“The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not the communion of the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is it not the communion of the body of Christ?”
1 Corinthians 10:16

Additional Scriptures for reflection: Matthew 26:26–28, 1 Corinthians 11:23–29, and Isaiah 53:4–5.
