=== TITLE ===

The Waymaker Angel of the Lord

=== SHORT INTRODUCTION ===

An encounter in which the Lord revealed His Waymaker Angel, and what it
means to partner with the angelic hosts in this hour.

=== THE WORDS ===

In an encounter with God yesterday, I saw this Angel of the Lord. I asked the Lord who he was, and He told me he is "My Waymaker Angel".

Angels as ministering spirits serving the heirs of salvation:

Hebrews 1:13-14

13 But which of the angels has he told at any time,
"Sit at my right hand,
until I make your enemies the footstool of your feet?"

14 Aren't they all serving spirits, sent out to do service for the sake of those who will inherit salvation?

This passage establishes Jesus' supremacy over the angels. The Son reigns at God's right hand, and the angels serve God and minister to those who inherit salvation.

Two additional passages describe that service:

Psalm 103:20-21

20 Praise Yahweh, you angels of his,
who are mighty in strength,
who fulfill his word,
obeying the voice of his word.

21 Praise Yahweh, all you armies of his,
you servants of his, who do his pleasure.

Psalm 91:11-12

11 For he will put his angels in charge of you,
to guard you in all your ways.

12 They will bear you up in their hands,
so that you won't dash your foot against a stone.

This is a passage behind the description of God as a "way maker."

Isaiah 43:16-21

16 Yahweh, who makes a way in the sea,
and a path in the mighty waters,

17 who brings out the chariot and horse,
the army and the mighty man
(they lie down together, they shall not rise;
they are extinct, they are quenched like a wick) says:

18 "Don't remember the former things,
and don't consider the things of old.

19 Behold, I will do a new thing.
It springs out now.
Don't you know it?
I will even make a way in the wilderness,
and rivers in the desert."

I proceeded to ask God why the Angel of the Lord had a sledgehammer, and God told me "to strike the ground". He told me that when this angel strikes the ground, it will shake evil out of its place, essentially making the way of the Lord. Jesus tells us in scripture that when evil is cast out, you know the kingdom has come upon you.

I could see electricity running through the angel's body, and I knew this electricity was ramping up the angel's power for the strike. I also knew that when he would strike the ground, there was going to be a massive shockwave that goes out. It's important you don't look to the left or to the right, but keep your mind on things above! In the noise comes the sudden victory! Perceiving correctly is paramount for the advancement.

Scriptural reference for shaking the wicked out of their place:

Job 38:12-15

12 "Have you commanded the morning in your days,
and caused the dawn to know its place,

13 that it might take hold of the ends of the earth,
and shake the wicked out of it?

14 It is changed as clay under the seal,
and presented as a garment.

15 From the wicked, their light is withheld.
The high arm is broken."

God is speaking to Job out of the whirlwind, describing His authority over creation. Here, the dawn is pictured as taking hold of the earth and shaking wickedness from it. The surrounding verses emphasize God's power to bring light, expose what darkness conceals, and break the power of the wicked.

"Expose what darkness conceals". This is why it's important to not get caught up in what we are seeing and hearing, but to pray, decree a thing, and firmly establish God's will over what is being exposed. This is what I sensed about the shockwave - a wave of shock. The only way to shake evil out of its place is to expose it. It's what we do with the exposure that's important. Using wisdom will be of utmost importance. We know the enemy is in the gate of America.

Pray. Seek God about what you see and hear. Wait patiently for the Lord on instructions. This is an angel sent to bring freedom and liberty. As heirs of salvation and ambassadors for Christ, we must partner with the angelic hosts to see God's will through in the Earth. It's time to advance the Kingdom of God like never before. Jesus told us we would hear about all sorts of things regarding calamity, wars, rumors of wars, and the like. Those things are par for the course. We have dominion over the darkness. The Saints of God are going to move in much greater power and authority in the coming times. Throw off the limitations, and remember what Jesus taught about the Roman soldier and His authority.

The officer's servant was seriously ill. When Jesus agreed to help, the centurion said:

"Just say the word from where you are, and my servant will be healed."
- Matthew 8:8, NLT

He explained that he served under superior officers and also commanded soldiers himself. When he gave an order, those under his authority obeyed. Jesus praised his extraordinary faith, and the servant was healed.

The centurion recognized that Jesus' words carried the authority to accomplish what He commanded. His reasoning was that, just as soldiers responded to military authority, sickness would have to yield to Jesus' authority. Jesus did not need to travel to the house or physically touch the servant for His command to be effective.

This is what legislating from the heavens looks like. We now carry the same power as Christ. Many are already operating in this. We built our chaplaincy ministry this way. Our authority as Christians is only increasing, but you have to have understanding and healthy doctrine to flow in it. Now is the time to rise in powerful faith!

I described to AI what I saw in the vision of the angel, and it depicted the vision. This is around 85 percent correct of what the Lord showed me, to give you an idea.
