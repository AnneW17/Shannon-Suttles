A Letter to Our Kingdom Family
A three minute read
 

Hello Kingdom Family,

I am super excited to be writing you today about our updates. We are so thankful for each one of you and your families. We continually remember you in our prayers and believe that God is working all things for your good as you trust in Him and His goodness.

A little update about us. As a family, we have had so much supernatural movement. God has positioned us on the coast of North Carolina, on the ocean, for a season. We are excited about this incredible upgrade that is costing us significantly less than what we’ve been spending to travel in this season. This was a total miracle of the Lord that we are still undone over!

We have been displacing powers in each new region God has sent us after leaving our home state of Indiana a little over a year ago. The divine synchronicity we have walked in has been a true “bringing the Kingdom of heaven to the earth” experience. It certainly has not been without its challenges, but through every challenge, God has grown us in significant ways. I will write more details about these assignments in the future.

America’s Watchmen
 

I am writing this letter to invite you to our new America’s Watchmen group for a ten day prayer push to the big day we have been leading up to with the Nehemiah Alliance, and the call to the Sacred Assembly on the 31st of this month.

I also created this group to go beyond that sacred day of calling America to repentance and prayer, into the midterm elections on the third of November. Once we are past the midterms, we will reevaluate the group’s purpose with God to see where we should go from there. Our hope is that you join us for detailed prayer points, updates, and more.
