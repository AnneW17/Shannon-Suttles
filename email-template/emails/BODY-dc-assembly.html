A Restored Family
 

We also carry a testimony that makes this calling deeply personal. Jason and I know what it means to walk through profound brokenness and contend for our family’s restoration. We have experienced separation from family, painful battles, long seasons of waiting, and the faithfulness of a God who does the impossible.

Today, our restored family serves together. Our daughter, Annie Whaley, serves alongside us as Chief Legal & Operations Officer. The daughter with whom I was once separated through adoption is now helping us build the work God has entrusted to our family. That restoration is part of the hope we carry for America: we have seen God rebuild what seemed beyond repair in so many ways, and we believe He can do it in families throughout this nation.

The Chaplaincy
 

For more than seventeen years, our ministry has involved prayer, discipleship, strengthening leaders, and walking closely with people through difficult seasons. For approximately ten years, we opened our home to women and children in crisis. Through Firebrand Revivalists, we have equipped believers to hear God’s voice, stand faithfully in prayer, and become watchmen over their families and communities.

That same heart is bearing beautiful fruit through Jesus Loves the Little Children Chaplaincy, our school chaplaincy ministry.

We are now in our third year of this work and our second year officially serving in the school system in New Jersey through the chaplaincy. During the first year, we watched God turn around an entire fourth-grade classroom when those serving the children were struggling to find a way forward. Through supernatural prayer and the gifts of the Spirit of God, consistent care, and addressing foundational issues, we began seeing changed hearts, greater peace, and miraculous turnarounds in the students. If many of you recall, this was a deep part of my own story — the girl no one knew how to help. So when God gave us this assignment, I knew He was up to supernatural transformation, in the face of what everyone had deemed impossible.

Danielle and Marco Sanzari are training to become our Directors of Chaplains. They have walked with us for more than ten years, and over that time we have developed a very strong and loving relationship. That relationship has grown even deeper since we began serving together through the chaplaincy. We are grateful for their faithfulness, their shared heart for children and families, and the way they have continued to stand with us through the years.

When God gives us the release to expand, Danielle and Marco will help us raise up and equip chaplains to serve in more schools across the nation. We are allowing Him to establish strong foundations as we oversee this work from another state, learning through the practical situations, complicated ones, and responsibilities that arise.

Through the chaplaincy, we have seen the supernatural ministry of Christ at work. Jesus still sets people free, brings deliverance, restores hope, and breaks the power of fear, confusion, oppression, and despair. His authority is not limited by geography, circumstances, or physical proximity.

For years, God has been teaching us to legislate in the heavens — to stand on His Word, pray in agreement with His will, and exercise the authority He has given us in Christ from wherever we are. This was foundational when we began pioneering prophetic ministry on social media while Facebook was still relatively new. We continue seeing how powerful that authority is, and how prayer reaches beyond our physical location with Jesus Loves the Little Children.

There is much more taking shape that we will share in future emails.

Ember Ridge
 

Our vision for Ember Ridge is a house devoted first to worship and prayer — a place where weary leaders and ministry families can rest, receive care, and be strengthened to return to their assignments. Hospitality, shared meals, discipleship, and sustained intercession are central to that vision. This is a vision that is now in motion, waiting for the Lord’s fulfillment.

Generational Legacy
 

Through the Generational Legacy Foundation, we are developing a vision to help remove financial barriers that stand between children and safe, stable families, including needs connected to family restoration, kinship care, and adoption from foster care. Our family understands how much these situations can cost emotionally and financially, and we want to help other families move toward lasting stability.

These expressions of ministry share a common purpose: loving God, restoring families, strengthening leaders, and investing in the generations coming after us.
