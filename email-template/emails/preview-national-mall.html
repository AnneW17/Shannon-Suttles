From the National Mall
A two minute read, plus two short videos
 

Dear Family in Christ,

We are so grateful for the opportunity to have joined the Nehemiah Alliance for the Sacred Assembly on the National Mall in Washington, D.C. I want to share a testimony from that day and personally thank those of you who helped us get there.

With more than 200 leaders speaking that day, each of us was offered a small portion of time, and our messages had to be timed down to the second. My assignment was one minute, and God did not give me the message until the morning of the assembly. It was a lot of wrestling matches up to that moment!

When He did, He gave me a message that expressed His heart for America’s healing for the family unit, and that message fit that one minute exactly, down to the second. It was a beautiful reminder that we can trust His timing and His ability to give us precisely what we need when we need it.

It was estimated that more than 10 million people saw that message. We are deeply thankful for every person who contributed through prayer and financial support. Your partnership helped make it possible for us to be there and carry what God had entrusted to us. Thank you for standing with us.

A Word at the Threshold
 

I also want to encourage you to watch the next video in this letter, where I share a word of breakthrough concerning the threshold we are each standing at in this season.

As we prepare to move into the next place with God, we need discernment to recognize the enemy’s attempts to sabotage what the Lord is bringing forth. How we respond at the threshold matters. This is a time to remain attentive to His voice, obedient to His direction, and steadfast in prayer.

Please watch the video for the full message and direction for overcoming these attempts to hinder our progress. Then join me in the prayer as we stand in agreement for breakthrough and prepare to step into what God has prepared for us.
